What will momma give the little ones for cold and cough? No more ‘Tussin that’s for sure. Actually, nothing at all if they are following the FDA’s latest recommendation.

The FDA has declared over-the-counter cold and cough meds too risky for young children . For children under the age of two, “serious and potentially life-threatening side effects can occur.” The FDA is also considering stricter recommendations for older kids. Read more…
