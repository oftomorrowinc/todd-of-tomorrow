Wow! I just realized it has already been two weeks since my last day at Yahoo!

In the last two weeks I have:

Connected with tons of old & new friends in the startup world.

Helped Rahmin , Strauss and the Cloudspace crew get Unclasses launched.

Hosted an (un)bar-b-q — i.e. indoor bar-b-q that got rained out.  (Credit on this one goes to my wife.)

Helped John & Jared get a rockin’ new Zentact extension launched — seriously, if you use Gmail, stop reading and go check-it-out.  Find out what all of your friends are up to as you are sending them email.  Great stuff!

Had a killer 4th birthday party for my son at the San Francisco Zoo.  (Again, all credit on this one goes to Lissy.)

Took an idea we had been kicking around for a long time and worked with John and Tim @ Cloudspace to make it real by South by South West 2009.  Famery is now live — though we are still doing minor tweaks for an official launch tomorrow.  Feel free to send in your sightings of friends that use twitter to help us kick the tires.

Not slept much.  I’m amazed at how quickly my body jumped back into hacking sessions going to 3:30 a.m.

Tomorrow (er, later today) I fly to Austin, TX to attend SxSW Interactive .  Can’t wait to see everyone there!  Be forewarned, everyone I see will be sighted on Famery .
