Great turn out at the OpenSocial hack event at Six Apart. Over 130 people.

Looks like things are moving along with the OpenSocial platform, but they still have a ways to go. Hi5, Ning, Orkut and Plaxo all have sandbox or production implementations up and running. MySpace showed off how their apps will work, but won’t have their sandbox live for a few more weeks.

Ian , Chris and myself all made it from the MyBlogLog team. Our big question: Where are the rest of the Yahoos?? If you went, ping me. If you didn’t… This is important stuff people — get involved.
